Fonts bundled with Kerf.

Inter and Inter Display (Regular, Medium, SemiBold) are from the rsms/inter
release (https://github.com/rsms/inter), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. The full licence is in OFL.txt.

Inter is the body font; Inter Display is used for headings.

Space Grotesk (variable, Medium default) is from the google/fonts repository under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (see OFL-SpaceGrotesk.txt). It is the display option for the Text tool.

DM Serif Display, Great Vibes and Oswald Bold are bundled for the Monogram Maker's themes, each
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-DMSerifDisplay.txt, OFL-GreatVibes.txt, OFL-Oswald.txt).
Their sources are recorded in ../PROVENANCE.md.
